# What it covers

Nine deck **vocabularies** of the "French family" — all built from the
standard four-suit vocabulary of
[DECON-01](/references/decon-01-card-model.md), either whole, subset,
extended, duplicated, locally re-ranked, or emitted in a non-default order.
Each is fully determined by its definition.
