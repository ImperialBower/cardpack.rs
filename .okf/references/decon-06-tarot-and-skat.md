# What it covers

Two deck **vocabularies** that share no cards, suits, or ranks with the French
family ([DECON-05](/references/decon-05-french-deck-family.md)): **Tarot**
(78 cards, Major and Minor Arcana) and **Skat** (32 cards, German-suited).
Both are expressed entirely in terms of
[DECON-01](/references/decon-01-card-model.md)'s card model.

# Why they matter

They are the proof that the card model is vocabulary-agnostic — the same
property [extending decks](/decks/extending-decks.md) relies on.
